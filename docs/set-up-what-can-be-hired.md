Open **Settings** on the Hire page.

## Hire rules

- **Minimum notice (days)** and **How far ahead (days)** control how soon and how far in advance customers can request.
- **Email these addresses when a request comes in**, one per line.

Press **Save settings**.

## Groups and items

A **group** is a kind of thing you hire, such as a 3 tonne digger. Under **What can be hired**, press **Add a group** and enter its **Name**, a description, a **Price (text)** and its **Order**.

- **Turnaround after each hire (days):** each item is held this many days after it comes back, for cleaning, inspection or repair. Customers never see it.
- **Customers can hire this** switches the group on or off for requests.
- The price is only shown. No payment is taken.

Each group has **Items**, the individual machines you own. Add each one under the group.
