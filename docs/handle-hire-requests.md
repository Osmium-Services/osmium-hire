**Hire** manages whole-day equipment hire. Customers send a request, and you confirm it.

## The schedule

Open **Hire**. The **Schedule** colours each item's days: **Waiting for you**, **Confirmed**, **Turnaround** (held after a hire, for cleaning or repair), **Out of service** and **Closed**. **Today** jumps to today.

## Answer a request

In the **Hires** list each request shows its status, the days, the customer, what and which item.

Open a request to see the customer's details and to enter **Your notes**, which are never shown to the customer. The **First day (collection)** and **Last day (return)** must be days you are open. The days in between can be closed ones, because the item stays out.

**Delete** moves a hire to **Deleted Hires**, where **Purge All** removes them for good.

## Add one yourself

Press **New hire**. Enter the **Customer name**, **Phone**, **Email (to send confirmation)**, **What**, the **Item** and the dates.
